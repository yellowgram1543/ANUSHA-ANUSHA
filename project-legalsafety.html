Commercial legal contracts require a high degree of precision, yet traditional RAG pipelines often suffer from structural loss (chunking boundaries breaking clauses) and silent hallucination. A zero-recompute, structure-aware RAG pipeline and NLI-grounded hallucination verification gate was required.

  • Structure-Aware Parsing: Hierarchical clause-boundary parsing that preserves semantics and character offsets.
  • Zero-Recompute RAG: In-memory FAISS indexing with BGE-small embeddings for sub-millisecond search.
  • Local Gen-AI Synthesis: Runs Qwen2.5-0.5B entirely on CPU, completely independent of external APIs.
  • NLI Verification Gate: DeBERTa-v3 cross-encoder validates generated claims to proactively block hallucinations.
