Developed a secure blockchain-anchored platform designed to combat document forgery and digital evidence tampering. It creates an immutable "Birth Record" for critical digital assets—such as academic certificates, legal records, ownership documents, and identity credentials—by combining AI forensics, cryptographic trust infrastructure, and decentralized verification.

  • Secure backend with Bull Queue
  • Vertex AI (Gemini Flash) forensic auditing
  • 3-tier PKI certificate authority hierarchy
  • Blockchain proof-of-existence anchoring on Polygon L2
  • OCR + AI vision validation
  • HMAC-SHA256 APIs & Tailwind CSS glassmorphism UI
